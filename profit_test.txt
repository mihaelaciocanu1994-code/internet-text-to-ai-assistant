COMPARISON
==================================================================
All four runs gave the correct answer, EUR 930.46. On this problem there was
no difference in accuracy between low and high effort, or between "answer
only" and "show the calculation". The difference was in the output: the
high-effort run explained its choices more (e.g. keeping EUR 57.112
unrounded because only the final amount should be rounded) and presented the
result as a table. A large current model handles this multi-step
calculation even with little thinking; smaller or older models are more
likely to make mistakes when asked for the answer only.
